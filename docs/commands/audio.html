Play Music / Play Sound / Stop Audio

Son of Fungus provides three audio-related commands for adding music and sound effects to your project. Audio files must be placed in the /audio folder so the runtime can locate them.

Play Music

Play Music starts looping background music. If music is already playing, it is replaced by the new track.

FieldDescription
Audio File The music file to play (e.g. theme.mp3).
Loop Whether the track loops continuously (default: true).

Play Sound

Play Sound plays a one-shot sound effect. Unlike music, sounds do not loop and multiple sounds can overlap.

FieldDescription
Audio File The sound file to play (e.g. click.wav).

Stop Audio

Stop Audio stops all currently playing audio — both music and sound effects.

Example

Audio commands in a block's command list:

☰ Play Music dungeon_ambience.mp3
☰ Say "You descend into darkness..."
☰ Play Sound door_creak.wav
☰ Stop Audio
Tip
When you build your project, audio files are automatically included in the output ZIP.
